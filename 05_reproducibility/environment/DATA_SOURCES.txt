Reproducibility archive for visual impairment, male sexual health and endothelial transcriptomic validation

Public data sources:

GSE206528: human corpus cavernosum single-cell RNA sequencing dataset.
GSE261085: human corpus cavernosum spatial transcriptomics dataset.
GSE2457: rat diabetic penile tissue expression microarray dataset.
ED GWAS: erectile dysfunction genome-wide association summary statistics.
OpenGWAS dataset identifier: ebi-a-GCST006956.

The GEO datasets can be downloaded from the NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus.
The GWAS summary statistics were obtained from the OpenGWAS resource.

The processed result tables correspond to the analyses reported in the manuscript.
The R package versions and session information are provided separately.
